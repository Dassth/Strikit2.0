Developed by Back Moon Devs

Strikit is proudly built and maintained by Back Moon Devs, a technology studio specializing in conversational AI and automation solutions. Our team recognized that millions of sports enthusiasts in India struggle with fragmented booking processes — calling multiple turf facilities, navigating clunky websites, or downloading yet another app. We asked a simple question: what if you could book a turf as easily as texting a friend?

The result is Strikit — an intelligent WhatsApp bot that understands natural language, provides real-time turf availability, handles payments securely, and sends instant confirmations. Built on robust conversational AI infrastructure, Strikit serves as a bridge between players and turf owners, operating entirely within the WhatsApp ecosystem that billions of people already trust and use daily.

Today, Strikit connects thousands of players with premium turf facilities across India. For turf owners, our bot provides automated booking management, reducing no-shows and maximizing occupancy. Whether you are organizing a weekend cricket match, a competitive football league, or a casual badminton session — just message Strikit and your game is on.
