Who We Are

About Strikit

Strikit was born from a simple yet powerful idea — every sports enthusiast deserves easy, instant, and reliable access to quality playing facilities.

Our Mission

To revolutionize how India books and experiences sports facilities by building a seamless digital bridge between turf owners and sports enthusiasts, ensuring every game starts on time, every time.

Our Vision

To become India's most trusted and widely-used sports infrastructure network — empowering millions of players to find, book, and enjoy world-class turf facilities across every city and town.

Our Values

Transparency, fairness, safety, and community drive everything we do. We hold both turf owners and users to the highest standards, ensuring a premium and trustworthy experience for all.

Our Story

Founded in 2026, Strikit emerged from the frustrations of avid sports players who spent hours calling turf facilities, only to find them fully booked or poorly maintained. We saw an opportunity to modernize the entire turf booking ecosystem — creating a platform where users can browse verified facilities with real photos, transparent pricing, and genuine reviews, while turf owners gain a powerful digital presence to manage bookings, reduce no-shows, and grow their business.

Today, Strikit connects thousands of players with premium turf facilities across India. Our platform features real-time availability, instant booking confirmation, secure online payments, and a robust rating system that rewards quality and accountability. Whether you're organizing a weekend cricket match with friends, a competitive football league, or a casual badminton session, Strikit ensures your game is always on.

We are more than just a booking platform — we are building a community of passionate sports enthusiasts united by the love of the game. Every booking made through Strikit contributes to a healthier, more active India.
