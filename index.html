How Strikit Bot Works

Book in 3 simple steps, all inside WhatsApp. No downloads, no sign-ups.

1

Message the Bot

Send "Hi" to the business's WhatsApp number. The bot replies right away and opens the booking form.

2

Pick Your Slot

Choose a date and see which slots are free. Pick your slot or appointment time and review the total.

3

Pay & Confirm

Pay online through the payment link, or choose to pay at the venue where the business allows it. You get an instant confirmation and a ticket link.

Who We Are

About Strikit

Strikit started as a WhatsApp booking bot for sports turfs. It now works for turfs, clinics and other slot-based businesses. No app, no website, just a WhatsApp chat.

Our Mission

Make booking a slot or appointment as easy as sending a message, on the app people already use every day.

Our Vision

Our Values

Keep it simple, be clear about prices and rules, and keep the bot reliable.

Developed by Back Moon Devs

Strikit is built and maintained by Back Moon Devs, a small freelance team in Sivakasi, Tamil Nadu (MSME registered), working with clients across India. Strikit is a separate product team within Back Moon Devs.

Each business gets its own WhatsApp booking bot on its own number. Customers pick a date and slot in the chat, pay online or at the venue, and receive a confirmation with a ticket link. The business gets an alert for every booking.

Strikit began with sports turfs and that is where it is live today. The same booking flow can be set up for clinics, salons, studios, gaming cafes, private theatres and similar businesses.

Why Choose Strikit

Bot Features

What the bot does for customers and for the business, all inside WhatsApp.

Instant Replies

The bot replies within seconds, any time of day.

Live Slot Availability

Customers see which slots are free. A slot that is already booked cannot be booked again.

Online Payment Links

Pay through a payment link sent in the chat. Card and UPI details are entered on the payment gateway's page, not in WhatsApp.

Confirmation and Live Ticket

After booking, the customer gets a confirmation message and a ticket link. The ticket page shows the current status and can be downloaded as a PDF.

Owner Alert on Every Booking

The business gets a WhatsApp alert when a booking is made or paid.

Simple Booking List

The business can see today's bookings in WhatsApp and open a booking dashboard with a one-time sign-in link.
